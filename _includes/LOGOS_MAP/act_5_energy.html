Theorem RG Isec:energy-symmetry

Energy Symmetry Law

\[ \boxed{\;\Delta E_{A \to B} \;+\; \Delta E_{B \to A} \;=\; 0\;} \]

Any closed system conserves its relational energy ledger with an invariant zero sum of total transformations. Transitions between states of observers (per unit of rest energy) must always sum to zero. Nothing is created, nothing is destroyed.

Section RG Isec:energy_quantities

Specific energy difference between two states

\[ \Delta E_{A \to B} = E_{0}\!\left( \frac{\kappa_{X,B}}{\beta_{Y,B}} - \frac{\kappa_{X,A}}{\beta_{Y,A}} \right) \]
\[ \Delta E_{B \to A} = E_{0}\!\left( \frac{\kappa_{X,A}}{\beta_{Y,A}} - \frac{\kappa_{X,B}}{\beta_{Y,B}} \right) \]

The energy of a system is determined by the ratio of the potential phase to the kinematic phase, \(E = E_{0}\,\kappa_{X}/\beta_{Y}\). The specific energy difference perceived for a transition between two states is determined by the difference in their total phase scaling.
