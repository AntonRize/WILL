Method

Independent research in the foundations of physics by Anton Rize, published openly as it develops. It is derived from four methodological constraints:

  • Epistemic hygiene. No unjustified assumptions are imported.
  • Relational origin. Physical quantities are defined by their relations. No background is allowed.
  • Ontological minimalism. The fewest possible primitives.
  • Mathematical transparency. No ontologically vague mathematics.

No new postulates, no free parameters and no arbitrary choices.

Tested against datasets

  • S2 star orbit around Sgr A*, 24 years of astrometric and spectroscopic data
  • SPARC galaxy rotation curves, 175 galaxies
  • Planck 2018 CMB acoustic spectrum
  • Pantheon+ Type Ia supernovae
  • Gaia DR3 wide binaries

Everything is public and reproducible: full derivations, runnable Colab notebooks and Desmos projects.

A note to the reader

Engaging with unknown independent research is a substantial commitment, and you should not do it blindly. Define the criteria this work would have to meet to justify your time, then put them to WILL AI above. Trained on this papers it will work as advance search engine, within its limits, and will link you straight to the relevant sections, notebooks and interactive web-tools.
