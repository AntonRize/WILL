# Selected Conversations

These are conversations with **WILL AI**, the assistant trained on the WILL
Relational Geometry corpus, that go deep enough to be worth reading on their
own. Each one has been chosen by hand.

They often work through material that otherwise appears only inside the
PDF papers: the reasoning behind a derivation, the objections it has to
survive, and the places where a result is stated as unfinished.

<div class="bg-gray-900/50 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 p-5 rounded-r-lg my-8" markdown="1">
**A note on what these are.** The answers are generated by a language model
working from the research corpus. They are a way into the material, not the
material itself. Where a conversation and a paper disagree, the paper wins.
The primary sources are the [papers, appendices and runnable
notebooks]({{ '/results/' | relative_url }}).
</div>

## Why only some conversations appear here

Anyone can talk to WILL AI, and every one of those exchanges is written to a
log. Those raw logs are kept public for transparency, but they are deliberately
excluded from search engines: they are unreviewed, frequently duplicated, and
sometimes contain nothing more than a greeting.

A conversation only appears on this page after being read and chosen. That
keeps the published set worth someone's time.
