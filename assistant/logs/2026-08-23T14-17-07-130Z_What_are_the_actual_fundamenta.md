The framework starts from:

1.  **Methodological Principles:** These are not ontological axioms but epistemic rules to prevent the introduction of unobserved absolutes. They comprise:
    *   [Epistemic Hygiene](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#pr:epistemic): Refusal to import unjustified assumptions.

    *   [Ontological Minimalism](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#pr:minimalism): Maintain the minimum number of ontological assumptions; absolute backgrounds are excluded.
    *   [Mathematical Transparency](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#pr:mathematical): Every mathematical phrase is a binding ontological statement, preventing obscure constructs.

2.  **Derived Foundational Theorems:** These are the deductive consequences of applying the methodological principles:
    *   [Relational Closure](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#thm:relational_closure): Any stable, self-contained system must be described by a closed and self-consistent relational geometry.
    *   [Causal Continuity (Conservation)](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#thm:causal_continuity): Within any closed dynamical system, any change must be perfectly balanced by a complementary change. This invariant measure of change is assigned the label "Energy".
    *   [Relational Isotropy](https://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#thm:isotropy): No spatial direction or coordinate can be \textit{a priori} privileged in a background-free system.

These foundational elements lead directly to the **Unifying Ontological Principle**:
$$\boxed{\textbf{SPACETIME} \;\equiv\; \textbf{ENERGY}}$$

In essence, WILL RG builds physics from pure relational necessities and algebraic closure, viewing continuum, metric, and differential formalisms as emergent, coordinate-dependent descriptions rather than fundamental ontological primitives.
