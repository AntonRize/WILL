folder"WHAT FOLLOWS FROM THEM"{
  "The body's own length. Compactness times size. This is the only thing about the body that the probe can feel."

  kappa_R=sqrt(10^(L_q))@{color:"#c74440"}

  R_s=kappa_R^2*R_b@{color:"#c74440"}

  "Where the probe is, and where it started, in metres."

  r=n_r*R_b@{color:"#000000"}

  r_0=n_0*R_b@{color:"#ffd700"}

  "DEPTH. The ratio of the two lengths, and the whole of what people call a gravitational field. It runs from almost nothing far away to exactly 1 at the red circle."

  kappa_o=sqrt(R_s/r)@{color:"#c74440"}

  "THE PART THAT IS HELD BACK. Fixed the moment the probe was released and never changes afterwards, however far the probe falls. This is the conserved quantity."

  beta=sqrt(R_s/r_0)@{color:"#2d70b3"}

  "THE PART THAT HAS TURNED INTO MOTION. Depth minus the held part. Every bit of depth the probe gains shows up here and nowhere else."

  beta_o=sqrt(max(kappa_o^2-beta^2,0))@{color:"#c74440"}

  "The same motion written as a speed, in metres per second and in kilometres per second."

  v_o=beta_o*c@{color:"#c74440"}

  v_k=v_o/1000@{color:"#c74440"}

  "ACCELERATION. The rate of the trade, rewritten per second of clock time. Not a cause of anything: a convenience."

  g=(R_s*c^2)/(2*r^2)@{color:"#ffa500"}

  "The same acceleration measured in Earth surface gravities."

  n_g=g/g_E@{color:"#ffa500"}

  "Distance in kilometres, for readability."

  r_k=r/1000@{color:"#000000"}

  "The depth the probe would reach standing on the surface. The full height of the bar on the right."

  q_sur=kappa_R^2@{color:"#c74440"}

  "Depth at the probe, as a plain number for the ladder."

  q_o=kappa_o^2@{color:"#c74440"}
