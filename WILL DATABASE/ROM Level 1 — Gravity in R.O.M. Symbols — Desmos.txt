folder"GLOBAL PROJECTIONS — fixed for the whole fall"{
  "kappa_sur is the potential projection at the body's own surface. R_s follows from it."

  kappa_sur=sqrt(10^(L_q))@{color:"#c74440"}

  R_s=kappa_sur^2*R_sur@{color:"#c74440"}

  "The probe's radius r, and the radius it was released from."

  r=n_r*R_sur@{color:"#000000"}

  r_rel=n_0*R_sur@{color:"#ffd700"}

  "GLOBAL KINETIC PROJECTION. A drop from rest is the degenerate R.O.M. orbit e = 1 with a = r_rel/2, so beta^2 = R_s/2a = R_s/r_rel. It is fixed the instant the probe is released and never changes again."

  beta=sqrt(R_s/r_rel)@{color:"#2d70b3"}

  "GLOBAL KINETIC PHASE FACTOR beta_Y = sqrt(1 - beta^2). Equals 1/(1+z_beta)."

  beta_Y=sqrt(1-beta^2)@{color:"#2d70b3"}

  "GLOBAL POTENTIAL PROJECTION, from the closure theorem kappa^2 = 2 beta^2.\nhttps://willrg.com/documents/WILL_RG_I.pdf#sec:closure"

  kappa=beta*sqrt(2)@{color:"#c74440"}

  "GLOBAL POTENTIAL PHASE FACTOR kappa_X = sqrt(1 - kappa^2). Equals 1/(1+z_kappa)."

  kappa_X=sqrt(1-kappa^2)@{color:"#c74440"}

  "GLOBAL RELATIONAL SPACETIME FACTOR tau = kappa_X * beta_Y = 1/Z_sys."

  tau=kappa_X*beta_Y@{color:"#6042a6"}

folder"LOCAL PROJECTIONS — at the probe, radius r"{
  "The subscript o means 'at the probe's position'. This is R.O.M.'s own convention."

  "LOCAL POTENTIAL PROJECTION. kappa_o^2 = R_s/r is the whole of what is usually called a gravitational field."

  kappa_o=sqrt(R_s/r)@{color:"#c74440"}

  q_o=kappa_o^2@{color:"#c74440"}

  "LOCAL POTENTIAL PHASE FACTOR."

  kappa_Xo=sqrt(1-kappa_o^2)@{color:"#c74440"}

  "LOCAL KINETIC PROJECTION, from the binding-energy invariant kappa_o^2 - beta_o^2 = beta^2. Clamped at zero outside the release radius, where the probe never was."

  beta_o=sqrt(max(kappa_o^2-beta^2,0))@{color:"#2d70b3"}

  "LOCAL KINETIC PHASE FACTOR."

  beta_Yo=sqrt(1-beta_o^2)@{color:"#2d70b3"}

  "LOCAL RELATIONAL SPACETIME FACTOR tau_o = kappa_Xo * beta_Yo. This is the clock rate."

  tau_o=kappa_Xo*beta_Yo@{color:"#6042a6"}

  "ESCAPE VELOCITY. v_e = kappa_o * c, at every radius, with no exceptions."

  v_k=(beta_o*c)/1000@{color:"#c74440"}
